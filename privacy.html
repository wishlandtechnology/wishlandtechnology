Last updated: June 2026 Data we collect and why Phone number: Used for one-time verification (OTP) when you sign in. We do not sell it or share it for marketing. Name and general location (city/area you enter at registration): Used to identify you in chat and match you with nearby users. Precise location (GPS): Used to match viewers with nearby publishers, announce live streams, and attach approximate location to uploaded replays. Location is sent to our servers when you use location-based features (request a stream, go live, upload a replay, or stay online as a viewer/publisher). Camera and microphone: Used while you broadcast live or record a video in the app. Live video/audio is streamed to our infrastructure and to viewers who joined the session. Recorded videos you upload are processed on our servers and may be published to YouTube for replay viewing. Optional stream description / chat messages: Shared with the other party during a live session to coordinate help. Device token (push): Used to notify publishers of incoming requests and viewers when a broadcaster goes live nearby. Live streams vs recorded replays Live streams are delivered in real time. We do not permanently store the live video on our servers after the session ends, unless you opt in to save the stream to YouTube. Recorded replays you upload are stored temporarily for processing (watermark, publish) and then published to our YouTube channel for others to watch. Title, description, and location may be shown to nearby viewers searching for replays. How we use it We use the data above to operate the service: verify identity, connect viewers and publishers, deliver live video/audio and chat, send push notifications, and publish optional replays. Sharing We do not sell your personal data. We may share data only as needed to run the service (e.g. cloud hosting, SMS for OTP, Google/YouTube for maps and replay publishing) or when required by law. Your choices You can decline permissions (location, camera, microphone), but some features will not work. You can sign out at any time. To remove your account or data, contact us using the support email or feedback link in the app About screen. Safety WireWhat does not verify other users. You are responsible for your safety and for respecting others' privacy. Do not share sensitive personal information in chat or on camera. Changes We may update this policy; the current version will be posted at the URL linked from the app and store listing.
